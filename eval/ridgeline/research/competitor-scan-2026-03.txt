competitor scan
march 2026
internal. not for the data room.
sources are mixed. crunchbase where it says so, otherwise "i looked at the site."

weski
package is the product. flight + stay + transfer, one checkout.
resort pages are thin. no weather on the resort tab.
revenue: i'm using $8M. pitchbook-ish, don't quote me. a blog said higher.
raised a little. not the story.

ski.com
person plans the trip. lodging on-page via expedia. lifts, sometimes rentals.
revenue: $15M. i had $20M in a note and $40M in another and i picked 15 because the middle felt wrong and 15 was in a 2024 article. low confidence.
they bought rocky mountain tours years ago.

onthesnow
search plus snow. app does favorite-resort alerts and cams.
1.7M visits/month was the crunchbase traffic note, with a big month-over-month jump. revenue on crunchbase is "<$1M" which does not match the traffic. leave both.

skiresort.info
filters for days. snow depth, slope length, the lot.
ui is a junk drawer. they make money on ads not checkout.
visits were small (25k) and then a crazy monthly jump. revenue <$1M crunchbase.

opensnow
app. forecasts, cams, avalanche, experts. subscription after a trial. not a booking site.
<$1M crunchbase. $3M seed back in 2022.

liftopia
lift tickets, then they bounce you to the resort. spotlio bought them.
raised $12.68M. valuation $21.5M in sep 2018. revenue band $1-10M. useless band.

mindtrip
not ski. chatbot, map pins, sends you away to book.
i asked it for a ski trip in february and got a beach. $20M raised, $55M post in 2024. ignore unless an investor brings up "ai travel."
